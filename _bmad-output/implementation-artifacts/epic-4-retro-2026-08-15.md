## What Went Well

### 1. Single Review Round Per Story — Unprecedented Efficiency

Every Epic 4 story passed review in 1 round. Compare: Epic 2 hit the 5-round cap on every story, Epic 3 averaged 3.8 rounds. The compound effect of internalized rules, mature patterns, and focused story scope made first-pass quality dramatically higher.

### 2. Unhappy-Path Discipline Fully Internalized

The Epic 3 retro's biggest contribution — adding "Unhappy Path & Failure Mode Discipline" rules to `project-context.md` — paid off completely. Every Epic 4 story proactively implements best-effort patterns:
- `create_case_record()` can never crash the execution cycle
- Fast-path failures fall back to normal pipeline
- Embedding failures don't block case record persistence
- Config loading failures fall back to Helm defaults

Reviewers found zero systemic unhappy-path gaps (vs. 5/5 stories in Epic 3).

### 3. Zero Deferred Debt

All 16 review findings were resolved in-place. No deferred items. Compare: Epic 2 ended with 11 deferred items, Epic 3 ended with 2. The codebase exits Epic 4 cleaner than it entered.

### 4. Story Intelligence Chain Working Beautifully

Each story correctly references predecessors with exact function names, line numbers, and anti-pattern sections. No story duplicated work from a sibling. The accumulated 4-epic context makes story specifications self-sufficient.

### 5. Epic 3 Action Items Delivered

- Manifest generation pipeline stage (action item #4) → became Story 4.0, delivered
- Unhappy-path rules → internalized by all Epic 4 stories
- Story close-out → all Epic 4 story files have complete records

## What Didn't Go Well

### 1. Four Pre-Existing Test Failures Never Fixed

`test_tools.py::TestQueryClusterResources::test_uses_get_resource_for_named_queries`, `TestGetResourceLogs::test_returns_evidence_with_logs`, and 2 `TestFreshnessGateNode` tests have been failing since before Epic 4. Every story noted them as "pre-existing and unrelated." Nobody fixed them. This erodes trust in the test suite.

### 2. Story 4.3 Had 3 Significant Logic Bugs in Core Flow

All caught in review, but the fast-path module had less careful implementation than the other three stories:
- `diagnosis_object_id` overwrite destroyed provenance traceability
- Multi-incident RCE siblings not transitioned (stuck in `queued`)
- Unchecked state transitions after initial gate

Root cause: Story 4.3 is the most complex story in Epic 4 — it orchestrates state transitions across multiple incidents, creates synthetic artifacts, and runs dry-run/policy-gate inline. Cross-cutting complexity still produces more bugs.

### 3. UAT Conditional Pass

The live cluster UAT validated schema, APIs, and fast-path decision logic, but could not observe:
- Case record creation after successful outcome (pipeline fails before outcome due to LLM limitations)
- Vector embedding generation (embedding model access denied)
- Fast-path replay on a second identical alert

Environment limitations (LLM rate limits, structured output reliability), not code defects. Being addressed separately.

### 4. Same-Model Review Trade-off

Opus 4.6 reviewing Opus 4.6 may have less diverse perspective than Epic 3's cross-model pairing. However, it also had fewer communication friction issues. The 3 bugs in Story 4.3 suggest same-model review still catches critical issues.
