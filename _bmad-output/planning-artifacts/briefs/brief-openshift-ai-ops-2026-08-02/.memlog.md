- (decision) Brief purpose: internal pitch to leadership, must also satisfy technical curiosity. Dual audience: decision-makers (why, value, scope) + engineers (how, stack, architecture). Source: brainstorm session output.
- (decision) Primary audience: SREs/ops engineers on-call. Secondary: platform teams. Users understand AI conceptually but are NOT assumed to run OpenShift AI — LLM is external, any endpoint works.
- (decision) Why now: Frontier LLMs + solid knowledge bases now surpass traditional dashboards+alerts+human-diagnosis model. The tipping point is here. AI ops initiatives are proliferating across large IT orgs at every level — this focuses the approach specifically on OpenShift as a product.
- (decision) Success criteria: (1) SRE team trusts auto-remediation for at least some alert types, (2) 90% correct diagnosis rate. Key risk: over-trust — tool can break things. Mitigation: eval harness simulating alerts and validating tool responses before production trust.
